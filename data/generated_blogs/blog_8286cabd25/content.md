## Before you book: a due-diligence checklist

- [ ] Verify property title and RERA registration at the Sub-Registrar Office.
- [ ] Confirm occupancy permit and building approvals from GHMC (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation).
- [ ] Check for clear land title and encumbrance records at the Registrar's office.
- [ ] Verify approvals for the specific project from the Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA).
- [ ] Confirm possession date and completion status with the developer's sales team.
